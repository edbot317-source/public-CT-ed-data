# Assumptions log -- test-score simulation run 20260916_110727

- Scenario: **ma**, changes take effect from FY2019; hold-harmless mode alliance; phase-in multiplier 1.0; slider overrides none; foundation index cpi; pass-through 100% in non-Alliance towns (100% Alliance/PSD); dose linear ramp to 4 years, plateau after.
- Shock: scenario ECS entitlement minus enacted, per ECS resident student, by fiscal year (from the dashboard's engine and data, ecs_dash_data.json). Treated as an OPERATING (noncapital) spending change spent one for one; no capital component. Partial-equilibrium: no behavioral responses (enrollment, local tax offsets, teacher labor markets) beyond what the reduced-form estimates embed.
- Dollars: nominal fiscal-year dollars deflated to 2018 dollars with CPI-U annual averages (FRED CPIAUCSL); fiscal year t uses calendar-year t CPI; years past the last complete CPI year (2025) use that year.
- Effect size: beta = 0.0343 student-level SD per $1,000 per pupil (2018$) at four years of exposure (Jackson & Mackevicius 2024, AEJ: Applied 16(1), Table 3 col 2 constant = noncapital marginal effect; se 0.00681, tau 0.0211). The pooled headline 0.0316 (col 1) is not used.
- Exposure: no churn since kindergarten; exposure = min(policy year, grade + 1). Dose linear to 4 years and flat after (user assumption). With a year-varying shock the effect is beta/4 times the sum of the last min(exposure, 4) years' per-pupil increases (reduces to beta x dose x delta for a constant shock).
- Horizon: FY2019-FY2025 (the dashboard's ECS panel and SEDA scores both stop at 2025), so no policy year exceeds 7; the plateau never binds.

Towns: local boards by name; K-12 regional districts (10, 12-18, 20; 6 through 2024) mapped to member towns; charter/magnet/RESC not assigned. Matched towns cover 99.3% of ECS resident students.
